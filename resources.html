Resources

Places you can go for support, information, or someone to talk to.

Crisis / urgent (Canada)

  • Call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline - 24/7, Canada)
  • Text CONNECT to 686868 (Kids Help Phone - 24/7, youth)
  • Call 911 for immediate danger or medical emergency

School help

  • Ask teachers for extensions or modified workload if stress is affecting you. It is okay to ask.
  • Talk to Guidance about timetable changes if you are overloaded.

Faith / culture / community

If you’d rather talk to someone who understands your background, culture, or faith, ask a trusted adult at school privately and they can help connect you. You do not have to explain everything in front of other students.

This site is not monitored 24/7. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call 911 or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, 24/7 in Canada).
