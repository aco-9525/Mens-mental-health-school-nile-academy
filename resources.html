School Supports

Ask teachers for extensions or adjusted workload if stress is affecting you. You’re allowed to ask.

Guidance can help change your timetable if you're overloaded.

Community / Culture / Faith

If you want to talk to someone who understands your background, culture, or faith, ask a trusted adult at school. They can connect you privately. You do not have to explain everything in front of other students.

This site is not monitored 24/7. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call 911 or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, Canada, 24/7).
