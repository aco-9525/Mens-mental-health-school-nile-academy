Pick what sounds most like you. You don’t have to be 100% sure.

🔥 Angry / irritated all the time

Anger can be stress, burnout, or feeling disrespected. You’re not a problem. Anger is a signal.

What you can do:

  • Try walking away before reacting and talk it out instead of holding it in.
  • Physical activity can help process anger.
  • Ask yourself: What am I actually frustrated about?

Talk to: Guidance Office | Mental Health Contact Teacher

😞 Sad / numb / no energy

Feeling empty, tired, and not enjoying anything can be a sign of depression. You don’t have to prove it’s serious to ask for help.

What you can do:

  • Move your body a bit, even for 5-10 minutes. Then talk to someone.
  • Tell someone: “I’ve felt off for a while and I don’t know why.”
  • Small steps count. Getting support is already progress.

Talk to: School Counsellor | Mental Health Contact Teacher

💭 Anxious / stressed / overthinking

Racing thoughts, tight chest, stomach knots, thinking about everything that could go wrong.

What you can do:

  • Box breathing: in 4 sec, hold 4 sec, out 4 sec, hold 4 sec. Repeat 4 times.
  • Break work into one task, not “fix my whole life tonight.”

Guidance can help with workload and schedule, not just “feelings.”

🙈 I feel alone. I don’t think anyone cares

Feeling isolated is serious. Being alone with heavy thoughts is dangerous, especially for guys who don’t usually open up.

What you can do:

  • Tell someone: “I don’t feel okay being alone right now.” That is a valid thing to say.
  • Reach out to 988 or 686868 if it’s late and you’re not at school.

This site is not monitored 24/7. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call 911 or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, Canada, 24/7).
