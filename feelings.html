How I'm Feeling

Pick the one that sounds most like you. You don’t have to be 100% sure. You just have to start somewhere.

I feel angry or irritated all the time

Anger can come from stress, burnout, feeling disrespected, or feeling like you have zero control. Anger is a signal, not proof that you're “a problem.”

What might help:

  • Walk away for 2-3 minutes before reacting so you don’t say something you regret.
  • Talk it out instead of letting it build and explode later.

You are allowed to ask an adult at school for help with anger. Asking for support does not make you weak.

I feel sad, empty, or tired

Feeling numb, not enjoying stuff you used to, or having zero energy can be a sign of depression. You do not have to “prove” it’s serious to get help.

What might help:

  • Move your body a little (walk, stretch) even if you don’t feel like it.
  • Tell someone: “I've felt off for a while and I don't know why.” That sentence is enough to start support.

I feel anxious / stressed / overthinking

Racing thoughts, tight chest, stomach knots, thinking about everything that could go wrong.

What might help:

  • Box breathing: in 4 sec, hold 4 sec, out 4 sec, hold 4 sec. Repeat 4 times.
  • Break work into one small task, not “finish everything tonight.”

Guidance can also help with workload and schedule, not just “feelings.”

I feel alone. I don’t think anyone actually cares

Feeling isolated is serious. Being alone with heavy thoughts is dangerous, especially for guys who don’t usually open up.

What might help:

  • Tell someone: “I don’t feel okay being alone right now.” That is a valid thing to say.
  • Reach out to 988 or 686868 if it’s late and you’re not at school.

This site is not monitored 24/7. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call 911 or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, 24/7 in Canada).
