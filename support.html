Privacy

Most of what you share stays private. Staff only has to report if someone is in danger, or if you are in danger. That’s the rule.

How do I even start?

You can say:

  • “I don’t feel okay.”
  • “Things are hard at home.”
  • “I’m overwhelmed with school.”
  • “Can I talk to you for 5 minutes?”

You do NOT have to tell your whole story right away.

This site is not monitored 24/7. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call 911 or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, Canada, 24/7).
