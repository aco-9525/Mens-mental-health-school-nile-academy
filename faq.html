Real questions. Real answers. No judgment.
How do I know if I’m actually struggling with mental health?
▼
If your mood, sleep, focus, or motivation changed and it’s been like that for a while, that matters. You don’t have to wait until you “break.”
What if I don’t want my parents to know?
▼
You can still talk to a teacher or guidance counsellor. You can also text crisis services anonymously (988 or text CONNECT to 686868). You don’t have to give your name. School staff will respect your privacy unless you’re in danger.
Is it normal for guys to feel emotional or want to cry?
▼
Yes. Shutting down all feelings nonstop is what actually hurts people long-term. Feeling things does not make you weak.
How do I help a friend who’s not okay?
▼
Ask privately: “Are you actually okay?” Then listen. If you’re worried about their safety, tell an adult. Saving someone is not “snitching.”
Will this go on my record or affect my future?
▼
Getting support is normal. Schools want you safe, not punished.
What’s the difference between feeling sad and depression?
▼
Sad = temporary reaction to something. Depression = longer, heavier, affects sleep/energy/motivation. Both deserve help.
