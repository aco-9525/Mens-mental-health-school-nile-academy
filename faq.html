Frequently Asked Questions

Real questions guys actually have. No judgment.

How do I know if I'm struggling with my mental health?

If your mood, sleep, focus, or motivation changed and it’s been like that for a while, that matters. You don’t have to wait until you “break.”

Is it normal for guys to feel emotional or cry?

Yes. Shutting down everything nonstop is what actually hurts people long-term. Feeling things does not make you weak.

What if I don’t want my parents to know?

You can still talk to a teacher or Guidance. You can also text 988 or 686868 anonymously. You don’t have to say your name.

How do I help a friend who’s not okay?

Ask privately: “Are you actually okay?” Then listen. If you’re worried about their safety, tell an adult. Saving someone is not “snitching.”

Will I get in trouble for saying I'm not okay?

No. The goal is to keep you safe and supported, not punish you.

This site is not monitored 24/7. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call 911 or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, 24/7 in Canada).
