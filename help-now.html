Get Help Now

If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself right now: you are allowed to ask for help immediately. Your safety matters more than anything else.

If you are in immediate danger:

• Call 911 right now.

• Tell an adult near you (teacher, office staff, coach). You do not have to explain everything. You can say “I don’t feel safe.”

Talk to someone 24/7 (free, confidential, Canada):

  • Text or call 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline - 24/7 in Canada)
  • Text CONNECT to 686868 (Kids Help Phone - 24/7 youth support)

You do not need to give your real name. You can literally start with “I’m not okay.”

Before you do something permanent:

  • Breathe for 60 seconds.
  • Move somewhere safer (not alone in a bathroom/stairwell).
  • Message one person who cares about you and say “I need you.”
  • Find an adult and say: “Can I talk to you for a minute?”

This site is not monitored 24/7. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about hurting yourself, call 911 or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, 24/7 in Canada).
